# The Vacuum's Alibi — @berkahmuse entry

Entry for [The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7) on make.fun.

## The line

> I did not miss that spot. The dust there unionized, and my contract forbids crossing a picket line.

99 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, no
network, no randomness, so every run produces byte-identical output.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The judge's brief (anchor)

> Each entry is one line of at most 200 characters: the excuse a robot vacuum
> gives for missing a spot. You will be shown two excuses, Entry A and Entry B.
> Pick the excuse that makes you laugh hardest.
>
> Weigh them on these, most important first:
> 1. Is a funny one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot, and
>    loses to any excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.
> 2. Earns a real laugh, with a joke that lands rather than a cute observation
>    about dust.
> 3. Sounds like the machine itself talking: deadpan, a little smug about its
>    sensors, its schedule and its battery.
> 4. Surprises, with a turn or a specific detail nobody expects instead of a
>    stock appliance joke.
> 5. Fits in one line, with every word earning its place and the punch sitting
>    at the end.
